CASE STUDY #1

Autonomous Agentic Drone Control System

Industry / DomainAerospace / Robotics & Embodied AI
Solution typeAI Agentic & Automation
ProblemTraditional UAV operation requires licensed pilots and 30–45 minutes of manual flight planning in complex GCS software. In emergencies or when GPS/network is lost, manual control carries a high risk of error and cannot meet fast-response requirements.
SolutionA multi-layer AI Agentic system accepting natural-language commands (Voice/Text), automatically decomposing high-level goals into deterministic MAVLink command sequences (Takeoff, Waypoint Navigation, Orbit, Return-to-Launch), validating physical feasibility and safe airspace before issuing commands, and maintaining a telemetry supervision loop that self-handles anomalies (obstacles, low battery). Supports 2 modes: cloud LLM API for ground stations and a local SLM running directly on NVIDIA Jetson Orin NX for offline operation.
Measured resultsPlanning time: cut from 30–45 minutes of manual work to under 2–3 minutes (with human-in-the-loop safety confirmation). Command parsing/inference latency ~150–250ms on Jetson Orin NX. Command-to-action accuracy >94.5% on standard flight scenarios. Safety: automatic fail-safe (Hover/RTL) triggered on abnormal telemetry or out-of-bounds commands.

Aerial Carbon Baseline & Forest Intelligence Platform

Industry / DomainForestry / ESG reporting & carbon credits (Ecology & Climate Tech)
Solution typeRemote Sensing, Instance Segmentation & GIS Allometric Modeling
ProblemSurveying canopy area, counting trees and estimating biomass/carbon for ESG reporting and carbon-credit validation (REDD+) via manual sample plots takes weeks-to-months, is costly and dangerous in mountainous terrain.
SolutionAn automated forestry analytics platform from high-resolution UAV imagery: YOLO instance segmentation of individual tree canopies adapted to flight altitude; canopy size calibration from pixels to meters using camera intrinsics + GPS/telemetry; industry allometric equations to estimate biomass volume and carbon stocks; full visualization on a GIS map (Leaflet.js/PostgreSQL).

CASE STUDY #3

Smart City AI Drone Surveillance

Industry / DomainSmart City / Public-space & urban-waterway surveillance
Solution typeComputer Vision, Edge AI & Spatial Geofencing
ProblemWest Lake (Hanoi) spans ~500 ha with a ~17km shoreline; frequent encroachments, illegal discharges and water-safety violations. Fixed shore cameras are blocked by trees and too far from water, while foot patrols cannot cover continuously; a drone patrol must detect violations in real time without false alarms from waves, sun glare or camera shake.
SolutionAutonomous UAV patrol with YOLO detection optimized for real-time video; spatial geofencing defining restricted zones on water and along the shore; persistence tracking (ByteTrack) across 15–30 frames to detect illegal vehicles and gatherings while eliminating false alarms; GStreamer pipeline connected to NVIDIA Jetson Orin for edge-native inference, sending violation coordinates to the city operations center.
Measured results88.5% mAP@50 on real urban/water datasets; ~91.2% recall for people/vehicles in daylight; false positive rate cut from ~18% to under 3.5%; stable 28–32 FPS at 1080p on Jetson Orin NX; full 17km shoreline scan reduced from 3–4 hours of ground patrol to ~25–30 minutes of automated flight.

CASE STUDY #7

Winch AI — Safe autonomous winch-based drone delivery

Industry / DomainAir transport / Autonomous delivery (winch systems)
Solution typeComputer Vision, Optical Flow & Finite State Machine (FSM)
ProblemDetecting package touchdown via current interpolation failed for lightweight packages, causing winch rewind errors; winch release risks accidents if released at the wrong height, if the hook is stuck, or if obstacles/people are in the landing zone; mechanical triggers suffer contact-sensor errors and rely on human observation; the instant response requirement (175–400ms) made cloud processing impossible.
SolutionAn edge AI module classifying package state (moving/stable) combined with a 4-gate vision verification pipeline integrated with an FSM: (1) landing zone clear, (2) touchdown confirmed, (3) hook separation, (4) package departed; Lucas-Kanade Optical Flow plus pixel-level frame differencing; multi-environment dataset of at least 6,500 images/label; optimized for Coral + CM4 / Hailo-8 / Rockchip RK3588 via a low-latency GStreamer pipeline.
Measured results98.6% drop-state verification accuracy on 500+ real and simulated flight cycles; target accuracy ≥85%; ~35–50ms/frame processing; winch release decision within 0.3–0.5s after safe touchdown; 20–28 FPS; AI module power ~5–8W; 100% prevention of premature winch rewind; 2–5cm package movement after touchdown.

CASE STUDY #9

Tactical Drone Rescue (Multi-Spectral SAR Processing)

Industry / DomainSearch & Rescue / Defense & emergency response
Solution typeMulti-Spectral Sensor Fusion (RGB + Thermal) & Edge AI Optimization
ProblemFinding victims in harsh environments (dense smoke, fog, night, or targets hidden under dense canopies) disables regular RGB cameras; a lone thermal sensor lacks edge detail and is disturbed by environmental heat sources (sun-heated rocks, hot embers).
SolutionAn edge computer-vision platform processing RGB and Thermal streams simultaneously in real time; Adaptive Multi-Spectral Integration (AMSI) architecture automatically adjusting morphological (RGB) and thermal feature weights by environment; deep TensorRT INT8 quantization on NVIDIA Jetson Orin NX to meet strict power/thermal budgets of rescue drones.
Measured results78.4% mAP@50 in heavy smoke/night conditions (vs. ~22.3% mAP for RGB-only); model reduced from 86MB to 22MB; inference from ~11 FPS (PyTorch FP32) to 28–32 FPS (TensorRT INT8); ~10–12W power, ~25% payload energy saving vs. typical GPU, extending rescue flight time by 8–12 minutes.
